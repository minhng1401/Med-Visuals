The adult dentition

32 permanent teeth, 16 in each jaw. Each quadrant holds the same sequence from the midline back: two incisors, one canine, two premolars and three molars (dental formula 2-1-2-3).

Cut and tear
Incisors (8) and canines (4), single-rooted
Crush and grind
Premolars (8) and molars (12), with cusps
First to erupt
First molars and lower central incisors, about age 6
Last to erupt
Third molars, age 17–25, often impacted or absent
Upper teeth
Maxillary nerve (V2) through the superior alveolar nerves
Lower teeth
Mandibular nerve (V3) through the inferior alveolar nerve

Numbering

Universal (used in the US): 1 is the upper right third molar, counting across to 16 at the upper left, then down to 17 at the lower left third molar and across to 32 at the lower right.

FDI (international): the first digit is the quadrant, 1 upper right, 2 upper left, 3 lower left, 4 lower right. The second digit counts from the midline, 1 for the central incisor to 8 for the third molar. Tap a number badge in the chart to switch systems.
