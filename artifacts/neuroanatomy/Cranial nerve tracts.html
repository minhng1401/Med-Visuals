    Drag the model to rotate it, scroll or pinch to zoom, and right-drag (or Shift-drag, or two fingers) to pan. Play moves the camera to each structure as the nerve reaches it. Use ◀ Stage and Stage ▶ (or the arrow keys) to step yourself, or click any stage to jump there. Turn on “All nuclei” to see every cranial nerve nucleus in the brainstem at once: motor columns sit near the midline, sensory columns lateral.

    Schematic, not to scale. Positions are simplified to keep the paths readable; the brainstem is drawn upright and translucent so the nuclei show through.
