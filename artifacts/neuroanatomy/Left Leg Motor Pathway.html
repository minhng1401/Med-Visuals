Moving the left leg

One voluntary step, traced from the right motor cortex, around the basal ganglia loop, down the corticospinal tract and out to the muscles of the left leg. Pick a station to see its anatomy, then pick a disorder to mark where it strikes.

Localizing rule for a weak left leg

Stations 1–8Above the pyramidal decussation the lesion is on the right, opposite the weak leg.
Stations 9–13Below it, in the cord, roots, nerves or muscle, the lesion is on the left, same side.
Station 2Basal ganglia lesions cause abnormal movement, not weakness, and show on the opposite side.
